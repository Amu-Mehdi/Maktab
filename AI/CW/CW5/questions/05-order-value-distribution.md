# Problem 5: Analyzing the Distribution of Order Values

For the `OrderValue` column:

1. Draw a Histogram using **6 bins**.
2. Calculate the mean of the column.
3. Calculate the median of the column.

Based on the outputs, answer the following questions:

1. Is the mean greater than the median, or is the median greater than the mean?
2. Which of the following best describes the shape of the distribution?

   * Normal
   * Uniform
   * Right-skewed
   * Left-skewed
   * Bimodal
3. Which value in the `OrderValue` column causes the tail of the distribution to be stretched?
4. Are most orders close to the value `900`, or are they far away from it?
5. If the value `900` were removed, would you expect the mean to increase or decrease?
6. For reporting the typical order value, is the mean or the median more reliable? Explain your answer based only on the presence of the unusually large value.
