# Problem 10: Scatter Plot and Correlation

Draw a **Scatter Plot** between `DeliveryMinutes` and `CustomerRating`.

Then calculate the correlation coefficient between these two variables.

Based on the plot and the correlation coefficient, answer the following questions:

1. Is the correlation coefficient positive or negative?
2. As delivery time increases, does the customer rating generally increase or decrease?
3. Do the points approximately move from the bottom-left to the top-right, or from the top-left to the bottom-right?
4. Classify the strength of the relationship as **weak**, **moderate**, or **strong**.
5. Is the correlation coefficient exactly equal to `-1`?
6. Can we conclude that every customer whose order was delivered late gave a low rating?
7. Does this relationship prove that delivery time is the only cause of low customer ratings?
