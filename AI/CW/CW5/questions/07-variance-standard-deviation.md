# Problem 7: Analyzing Variance and Standard Deviation

Calculate the variance and standard deviation of the following columns:

* `OrderValue`
* `DeliveryMinutes`
* `CustomerRating`

Then answer the following questions:

1. If the standard deviation of a column is zero, what does this tell us about the values in that column?
2. Does the presence of the value `900` increase or decrease the standard deviation of `OrderValue`?
3. In the `CustomerRating` column, are the values relatively close to the mean, or are they spread over a very large range?
4. Why is variance always non-negative?
5. Why is standard deviation generally easier to interpret than variance?
6. Is it appropriate to directly compare the standard deviation of `OrderValue` with the standard deviation of `CustomerRating`? Explain your answer based on the difference in units and scales of the two columns.
